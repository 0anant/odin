Ingredients

  • Rice flour
  • Sugar or Jageri
  • Maida flour
  • Ghee
  • Milk

Steps

  1. 1.light up gas and boil ghee
  2. properly add the prepared mixture in a jalebi making tool
  3. make it cool in the tool for good time
  4. make proper jalebi shapes while in the boiling ghee
  5. cook it on light medium temperature for about 10-15 min
  6. making sure it cooked well add it in sugar syrup
